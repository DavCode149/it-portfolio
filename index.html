An IT Support Specialist helps people fix problems with computers and technology. They make sure computers, software, and internet work properly in schools, offices, and businesses.

Their job is important because lots of people need computers to do their work everyday. If something breaks, the IT Support Specialist fixes it so people can keep working.

A typical day might be answering HelpDesk tickets, checking computers for problems, installing new programs, and helping co-workers understand how to use their devices.

Daily Tasks
Help users, fix computers, and install software
Typical Day
Respond to HelpDesk tickets, Fix any errors or problems that may occur.

Education

Highschool students can start preparing for this career by taking technology and math courses. These classes help you learn how computers work and how to solve problems.

Some Highschool courses include Computer Science (ICS), Mathematics, Business Studies, and Communications Technology. These subjects teach students about computers, math, and other digital things.

Online Courses exist as well such as Google's Career certificates on Coursera, and other courses hosted on Coursera by Universities and Colleges.

After Highschool, you can go to college or university for Information Technology or Computer Systems classes. Some people also earn certifications like on Coursera to prove their skills. Education usually takes about two to four years to finish.

Skills

IT Support Specialists need technical skills such as understanding computer parts, software, and basic networks. These skills help them find and fix problems quickly. They also need strong communication skills, which is important because they may need to explain computer problems in simple ways so normies can understand them.

Personal qualities like patience, teamwork, and responsibility are also important. Sometimes users feel frustrated when their computer does not work, so the IT Support Specialist must stay calm and helpful.

Technical Knowledge
Communication
Problem Solving

Salary

In Ontario, an entry-level IT Support Specialist can usually earn between $40,000 and $50,000 a year. As they gain more experience, they can earn between $60,000 and $80,000 a year or more.

Most IT Support Specialists are paid a salary, but some can be paid hourly. The amount of money depends on where they work, how much experience they have, and what skills they know.

Environment

IT Support Specialists can work in offices, schools, hospitals, or businesses. They often sit at desks and work on computers for most of the day (My kind of thing).

Some IT Support Specialists work remotely and help people over phone or internet. This lets them to solve problems without being in the same building. Most work during the day, but some jobs require you to work evenings or being on call in case of emergencies.

Final Notes

This career is important because we depend on technology everyday. Without IT Support Specialists, businesses and schools would not be able to fix computer problems quickly and would have to stop using technology.

Some benefits of picking this career include high demand and many opportunities to grow into other IT-related positions. Some cons and challenges can include dealing with stressful situations and helping people who are upset/unreasonable.
