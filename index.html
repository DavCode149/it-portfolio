IT Support Specialist

An IT Support Specialist helps people and businesses solve computer problems and keep technology working properly.

Daily Tasks & Responsibilities

Fix computer and software problems
Install and update programs
Set up computers and networks
Help users with technical issues

Typical Day

A typical day includes answering support requests, troubleshooting hardware and software, and maintaining systems.

Education & Training (Ontario)

High School Courses

Computer Science (ICS)
Mathematics
Business Studies
Communications Technology

College / University / Certifications

Ontario College Diploma (IT or Computer Systems)
University Degree (Computer Science or IT)
Certifications (CompTIA A+, Network+)
Apprenticeships or Co-op programs

How Long Education Takes

Education can take 2–4 years depending on the pathway chosen.

Skills & Qualities Needed

Technical Skills

Computer hardware knowledge
Networking basics
Problem solving
Communication skills

Personal Traits

Patience
Teamwork
Responsibility
Attention to detail

Salary / Earnings (Ontario)

Entry-level: $40,000–$50,000 per year
Experienced: $60,000–$80,000+ per year
Usually paid salary
Varies by location and experience

Work Environment

Offices and schools
Hospitals and businesses

Work hours are usually daytime, but some jobs require shift work or on-call support.
