An IT Support Specialist helps people and organizations solve computer and network problems across Ontario.

A typical day involves responding to technical issues, installing software, and helping users.

Daily Tasks
Fix computers and assist users

Education (Ontario)

High school courses include Computer Science (ICS), Mathematics, Business, and Technology.

College and university programs focus on IT and Computer Systems.

Skills

Technical Knowledge
Communication
Problem Solving

Salary

Entry-level: $40,000–$50,000 per year.

Experienced: $60,000–$80,000+.

Environment

IT Support Specialists work in offices, schools, hospitals, and remotely.

Extras

This career is important because society relies on technology.

Fun fact: the first computer bug was a moth in 1947.
